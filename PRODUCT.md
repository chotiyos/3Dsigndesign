## Users

(Inferred from the reference product the owner pointed at; not yet confirmed.)
Thai sign shops, acrylic and plaswood fabricators, 3D-print hobbyists, and small shop owners who need
letter signs. They work on a shop desktop, between jobs, and want a file a machine can cut or print.

## Product purpose

JJDesign turns typed Thai or English text, an SVG, or a traced image into a parametric 3D letter sign,
split into real fabrication parts, and exports STL and DXF in millimetres. Thai shaping is correct because
HarfBuzz places vowels and tone marks from the font's own data.

## Capabilities and truth

- Runs entirely in the browser. There is no backend.
- Login, credits, top-ups, cart and checkout are browser-only demos and must be labelled as demos.
- The AI model page shows sample results only. It is not connected to any AI service.
